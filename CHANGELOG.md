# Changelog

User-facing changes, newest first. Your data is kept across updates: every
version so far only adds to what is stored, and older backup files still
restore.

## 1.1.0 (2026-11-12)

- Desktop and tablet layouts: a sidebar with the Banzir mark on computers,
  two columns on Today, balanced columns on Progress and Profile, a sticky
  summary beside Program, Food and Fasting, and centred dialogs. Hover
  feedback with a mouse or trackpad.
- Install instructions for computers (Chrome, Edge, Safari on Mac).
- Fixes: the coach opens on the latest message; macro bars line up; the
  weight chart fills its card.

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-12)

First release.

Training
- Onboarding that builds a plan for your goal, level, equipment and time:
  muscle, strength, calisthenics skills, running, fat loss, general fitness or
  sport support. Every step can be skipped, and a backup can be restored from
  the first screen.
- 35 ready-made plans, from first steps to advanced, including calisthenics
  skill plans (handstand, planche, front lever, muscle-up, first pull-up),
  running from couch to marathon, and plans for football, tennis, boxing and
  other sports.
- A plan builder: edit days, exercises, sets, reps, holds, intervals and rest;
  saved programs you can switch between.
- A guided workout player: one set at a time, rest timer, hold stopwatch,
  interval timer, last-time values, load and progression suggestions, swaps,
  screen kept awake, resume after a reload.
- Progress: weekly sessions, sets per muscle, personal bests, full history.

Optional modules
- Food: calorie and macro targets with safety rules, food log with Open Food
  Facts search, favourites and recents, water, calorie corrections from your
  weight trend.
- Body weight: weigh-ins, 7-day average and weekly rate.
- Running: run log, GPX import, heart-rate zones, race-time equivalents, the
  10 % rule for single runs.
- Fasting: eating windows, a fasting timer and log. Opt-in, and not offered to
  under-18s, during pregnancy or when underweight.

AI coach (optional)
- Bring your own key: Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, OpenRouter, Groq, Mistral or any
  OpenAI-compatible server, including a local model.
- Reads your plan and log, remembers notes you can see and delete, and
  proposes plan or nutrition changes that you accept or dismiss.

Look and feel
- Ten themes, any accent colour, three corner styles, kg or lb, km or mi.
- Accessible: WCAG 2.1 AA contrast in every theme and accent colour, labelled
  controls and landmarks, keyboard focus kept inside dialogs, reduced motion
  respected.

Your data
- Everything stays on your device. Backup and restore as one file, CSV export.
- Works offline once installed.
