Your own workout app. Free, open source, and yours to keep.

Banzir builds a training plan for your goal: muscle, strength, calisthenics
skills, running, fat loss or a sport. 35 plans, from your first push-up to
your first marathon.

- Guided workouts, set by set, with a rest timer and what to lift next time
- Progress you can see: weekly sessions, personal bests, body weight, runs
- An optional AI coach that reads your log, with your own key
- Food, running and fasting modules, only if you want them
- Ten themes, on phone and desktop, works offline

No account. No subscription. Your training data stays on your device.

Get your own copy in two minutes: fork it on GitHub, deploy it to Vercel,
open it on your phone. Link in bio.

Save this for your next training block, and send it to the person you train
with.
